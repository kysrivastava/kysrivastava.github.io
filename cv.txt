RNA-Fab interfaces characterization: Characterized binding kinetics and established structure- function relationship for RNA- Antibody fragment (Fab) interface by Biolayer interferometry (BLI) and SPR.
RNA: Protein Complex Crystallization: Optimized Fab construct and Crystallized an RNA-Fab complex, solved the complex structure to 2.7Å resolution.
Graduate Research Assistant                                                                                     2018 – 2024
Department of Biophysics, Structural, and Computational Biology
University of Rochester Medical Center 
Thesis Title: Structure and function analysis of bacterial riboswitches that control translation
RNA -ligand complex Crystallization: Crystallized and solved structure of four PreQ1-III Riboswitch mutants in complex with their ligands using SLACK SSRL beamline and tools such as Phenix, Coot, XDS and CCP4 suit.
Gene Regulation Analysis: Adapted and optimized a GFP based bacterial reporter assay to characterize the gene regulatory abilities of riboswitch mutants. 
Secondary Structure Analysis: Characterized secondary structure of four riboswitch mutants in solution using SHAPE-seq; used GraphPad Prism and Python for data analysis and data visualization.
Ligand Recognition Characterization: Characterized ligand binding thermodynamics and stoichiometry for NAD+-I riboswitches by testing individual domains and full-length riboswitch using Isothermal calorimetry (ITC) and Size Exclusion chromatography (SEC) to demonstrate promiscuous ligand recognition (Biochemistry, 2023).
Crystallization Module Development: Developed RNA crystallization and phasing module by engineering methionine residues into the U1A motif and lab-evolved RNA binding proteins.
Research Assistant, MS                                                                                                  2016-2018
Cloning and expression of membrane spanning protein: Cloned, expressed and purified sensory domain of a Zn sensing protein ZraS.
