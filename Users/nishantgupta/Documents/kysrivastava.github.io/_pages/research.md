My research is organized into three main threads:

## Thread 1: Riboswitch Structure/Function
Investigation of bacterial riboswitches that control translation through structural and functional analysis. Current work focuses on understanding how these RNA elements regulate gene expression by binding specific ligands.

**Key Projects:**
- Structure and function analysis of PreQ1-III riboswitch mutants in complex with their ligands
- Characterization of ligand binding thermodynamics and stoichiometry for NAD+-I riboswitches
- Development of engineered riboswitches with improved crystallization properties

**Techniques:** X-ray crystallography, SHAPE-seq, isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC), Size Exclusion chromatography (SEC), GFP reporter assays

## Thread 2: RNA-Ligand Recognition
Study of molecular recognition mechanisms between RNA and small molecule ligands, with emphasis on understanding the thermodynamic basis of binding specificity.

**Key Findings:**
- NAD+-I riboswitches bind a single co-factor but cannot discriminate against adenosine triphosphate
- Two riboswitches sharing a common ligand-binding fold show dramatic differences in mutation accommodation
- Promiscuous ligand recognition demonstrated in NAD+ family riboswitches

**Methods:** Affinity measurements, structural analysis, thermodynamic characterization, mutational analysis

## Thread 3: RNA/Fab Structural Biochemistry
Characterization of RNA-Antibody fragment (Fab) interfaces and development of novel structural biology approaches.

**Accomplishments:**
- Established structure-function relationships for RNA-Fab interfaces using Biolayer interferometry (BLI) and SPR
- Optimized Fab constructs and crystallized RNA-Fab complexes
- Solved complex structures to 2.7Å resolution
- Developed RNA crystallization and phasing modules through engineering

**Applications:** Structural biology tools, antibody engineering, RNA-protein interaction studies
